# Security

If you find a bug that could expose private messages, bypass permissions, or
compromise an account or server, please report it privately. Don't post exploit
details in a public issue or pull request.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email [support@cinnamon.mx](mailto:support@cinnamon.mx), or
[report privately through GitHub](https://github.com/CookieNom/KaedeChat/security/advisories/new).
You can also open the repository's **Security** tab and click **Report a
vulnerability**. Fill in the details and submit the report; it won't create a
public issue.

Include what you can:

- The affected component and version or commit.
- Steps to reproduce the problem, or a small proof of concept.
- What someone could access or do by exploiting it.
- Any setup details needed to reproduce it, such as federation or encryption settings.

Remove passwords, tokens, encryption keys, and other people's data from logs
and screenshots. You don't need a complete write-up to flag a suspected problem.

## Testing and disclosure

Use your own instance and test accounts, or get permission from the instance
operator first. Avoid tests that interrupt service or access other people's data.

Please give maintainers time to investigate and arrange a fix before publishing
the details.

For abuse, moderation, or an account problem on a particular Kaede instance,
contact that instance's administrators. Ordinary bugs can go in
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/CookieNom/KaedeChat/issues).
